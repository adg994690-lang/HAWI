How to use HAWI

A quick guide to exploring the menu

FoodBreakfastDrinks

1. Choose a category

Tap a category, such as Breakfast or Drinks, to narrow the menu. Use All to see every dish.

250 ETB

2. Search and explore

Scroll through the cards to see each dish's image, name, description, and price. Tap the search icon to find a dish by name (such as “kitfo”) or price (such as “250”). You can also use the quick price ranges or enter a minimum and maximum price, then tap Apply.

Dish

3. Save your favourites

Tap the small star at the bottom of a dish card. Open SAVED at the top to review your saved dishes and their total price.

Saved dishes stay on this browser. They are a reminder list, not an order.
